=== CMS Mobile Splash Redirect ===
Requires at least: 6.2
Requires PHP: 8.0
Stable tag: 1.1.0

Redirects phone visitors to The Scene after the landing-page welcome video
finishes. Desktop visitors continue to see the landing page.
